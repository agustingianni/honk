> honk

An IRC client for your terminal, built around the keyboard.

curl -fsSL https://agustingianni.github.io/honk/install.sh | sh

macOS and Linux (including Alpine), on Apple Silicon, Intel and ARM. One file, no runtime to install. The script checks the download's checksum and puts honk in ~/.local/bin, without sudo. honk tells you when a new version is out, and honk upgrade installs it. Read the script or download a binary yourself. Every release is built by GitHub Actions and signed, so you can check where it came from.

Start

Connect to a server and join a channel:

honk --server irc.libera.chat --nick your_nick --channel '#libera'

To connect to several servers when honk starts, write a config file.

Or look around first, without connecting anywhere:

honk --demo

What you get

  • Jump anywhere. Press Ctrl+K and type part of a channel, a DM or a nick. Commands are there too.
  • All your networks in one window. Several servers at once, with TLS by default, SASL login and bouncers.
  • Scroll back further. honk keeps a log of your chats, so each channel and DM opens with its last messages, even after a restart. Scroll up for older ones.
  • One conversation, every device. What you send from your phone or another client shows up here too.
  • Easy to follow. A color for each nick, mIRC colors, highlighted mentions, and a marker at your first unread line.
  • Know when someone wants you. A desktop notification for mentions and DMs, even over SSH.
  • Survive a dropped connection. honk reconnects as soon as the network is back, loads the DMs you missed, marks the mentions you missed, and shows which of your lines never reached the server.
  • Your colors. More than 60 color themes. Press Ctrl+K and type theme to try each one on the whole screen.

Keys

Ctrl+KOpen the omnibox
Alt+Up / DownPrevious or next channel or DM
TabComplete a nick or a command
Ctrl+FSearch your chat logs
Ctrl+BShow or hide the channel list
Alt+UShow or hide the user list
/helpList every command

Not yet

honk is young. Things it does not do today:

  • Windows.
  • An ignore list.
  • Moderation commands such as /kick, /ban and /mode. Send them with /raw for now.
  • Connecting to IRC through a proxy. honk upgrade does use HTTPS_PROXY.
  • DCC file transfers, scripts and plugins.

Missing something you need? Open an issue.
